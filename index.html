ABOUT THE DESIGNER

I’m Adeshina Amirah Titilayo, a graphic designer based in Ibadan, Nigeria. I create promotional flyers, menus, social media graphics and brand visuals for businesses and creative people.

Working with Canva and a careful eye for composition, I shape each piece around its message and purpose. I value clear communication, considered colour and details that help every design feel memorable.
